Reviewer 1: The paper's single load-bearing 'technical spine' — the design-effect-corrected posterior α_a=α₀+n_eff·p_a with n_eff the Kish ESS of the leverage weights — rests on a mathematical error. The Kish ratio of the weights measures weight *dispersion*, not the number of independent votes: under uniform correlation (all traces identical, or equal fully-correlated blocks) the weights are uniform and Kish returns K, so the posterior injects K independent-vote's worth of evidence for K identical copies. The coherent effective count is Σw_i=tr(D⁻¹S), which the spec even derives but does not use — and which collapses the 'novelty' back into the α₀+W_a form the authors reject. The same error propagates into the never-worse-than-SC guard, whose Kish-based ESSratio fails to fire on pure verbatim echo, contradicting the R4 experimental prediction the guard is built to satisfy. Secondary issues: the 'posterior mean matches weighted shares' and 'unique parameterization' claims are false for α₀>0; n_eff (from w) and p_a (from w·g(c)) use inconsistent weightings; and the top-2 Beta stability probability is mislabeled as P(leader is the mode) and undermines the exact-VoI/martingale claim. Novelties 1 and 3 are the strongest advertised contributions and both are broken by the same root cause. This needs the core estimator reworked and the S→all-ones / pure-echo cases added as falsification tests before it is defensible.
Reviewer 2: Reject as currently positioned. On the spec's own admissions and standard prior art, none of the four novelty claims survives. Claim 1 (the 'spine') is the Rao-Scott/effective-sample-size design-effect correction (survey statistics, 1981-84) and equivalently a one-line composition of two cited works (Kish-ESS 'Nine Judges' + ASC's Dirichlet posterior); its own 'three designs converged' defense argues obviousness. DDWC's 1/rowsum weighting is conceded to be fitness-sharing / inverse-density / a ridge-leverage surrogate. Claim 2's dup-vs-sem split is ordinary near-duplicate dedup and is inert on real temperature-0.7 traces where verbatim CoT echoes essentially never occur, collapsing the method to down-weighted-semantic approximately equal to SC. Claim 3 (min-conjunction + guard) is a generic conservative combination that architecturally reduces to SC/ASC on real data. Claim 4 (VoI/token) is textbook EVSI-per-cost / SPRT, conceded thin, with a misapplied nonnegativity argument. Compounding this, the min(P^raw,P^eff) design makes the stopping rule provably unable to stop earlier than ASC, contradicting the headline 'Pareto-dominates ASC.' The honest path is to publish this only as a rigorous negative/robustness result -- precisely the fallback the spec itself names in Limitation 8 -- after fixing the false expectation-matching math and the w-vs-v ESS inconsistency, and after reporting the real-data dup and guard-firing rates that determine whether any mechanism is even active.
Reviewer 3: The experimental design is dressed in the language of falsifiability (per-claim CONFIRM/FALSIFY tables, no-paper conditions) but has three critical validity holes that would sink it at review. (1) The 'fair test' simulated oracle is circular: it generates data from exactly the block-cluster / per-mode-copy-rate structure DDWC's math assumes, and R2/R4 hard-code the very correlated-wrong / adversarial-duplication situations the method is built to win -- so any sim 'win' is an artifact of the data-generating process, not evidence about real traces. (2) The headline calibration demonstration (naive alpha0+W_a is over-confident vs ESS-corrected) is true by mathematical construction for any data and cannot fail, making Novelty 1's confirmation vacuous. (3) The matched-budget protocol is under-specified in exactly the way that lets adaptive-stopping baselines be under-tuned: it never states that every baseline's stopping threshold is independently swept to trace its own frontier, and the win is a point-vs-point at 'matched mean cost' rather than frontier-vs-frontier. On top of these, the method carries ~12 tunable hyperparameters against SC/ASC's 0-1, with a garden-of-forking-paths across regimes x datasets x models x confidence-variants and no multiple-comparison correction or frozen default config, and the never-worse guard can be tuned per-regime to satisfy every falsifier at once. Concrete fixes: demote the sim to an implementation sanity-check and drive the empirical kernels from real cached traces; test calibration against a genuine competitor on real held-out ground truth, not a strawman; require each baseline to trace its own tuned frontier with identical overhead accounting; freeze one default config and correct for multiple comparisons; and add the 2x2 consensus-x-stopping factorial so the stopping machinery's marginal contribution is actually isolated. Without these the paper is 'three known pieces bolted together, validated on a simulator that assumes the conclusion.'
