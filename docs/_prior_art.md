(2) Applying redundancy-discounting to SC voting specifically is also published: VecCISC (2605.08070) clusters reasoning-embedding-similar traces to remove redundant/degenerate ones before confidence-weighted voting; 'Agreement in Representation Space' (2606.12003) and RECSIP (2503.12108) do embedding-space clustered voting; the confidence multiplier is CISC (2502.06233). The only arguably-unpublished detail is the precise soft, threshold-free continuous weight 1/sum_j S_ij in place of hard clustering — a trivial re-parameterization of fitness sharing. Closest prior work: Goldberg & Richardson 1987 (formula); VecCISC 2605.08070 (SC application). Net: not novel; incremental at best.

COMPONENT 2 (VoI-Stop, Dirichlet-multinomial posterior + P(leader stable)>=tau OR VoI/cost<lambda): ALREADY KNOWN in both halves (verdict b). The 'Dirichlet posterior over the answer distribution, stop when P(current leader stays the mode) >= tau' rule IS Adaptive-Consistency (Aggarwal et al. 2023, C_thresh=0.95). The seed's premise that Adaptive-Consistency uses 'raw counts, no Dirichlet, no stopping-probability' is simply wrong — that is precisely what ASC does. The VoI/cost half (stop when expected marginal risk reduction per token < lambda) is classical value-of-information / optimal stopping (Howard 1966; Wald SPRT; Weitzman 1979 'Pandora's box') and has ALREADY been ported to test-time LLM sampling in a crowded 2025-26 literature: Pandora's-box optimal stopping (2510.01394), Optimal Bayesian Stopping (2602.05395), anytime-valid CITE (2605.05873), SPRT-based ConSol (2503.17587), risk-controlled MARS (2606.12935), Difficulty-Adaptive SC (2408.13457). Closest prior work: Aggarwal et al. 2023 (Dirichlet stopping); the 2025-26 optimal-stopping cluster (cost/VoI objective). Net: not novel.

THE COMBINATION (redundancy-discounted / weighted counts fed into a Dirichlet stopping rule with an explicit VoI-per-token objective): NOVEL COMBINATION (verdict c), but weak. I could not find this exact fusion published, so the honest claim is 'novel integration,' not 'novel method.' Two caveats make even the combination fragile: (i) each ingredient is individually standard and the coupling is mechanical, so it reads as engineering; (ii) VecCISC + Adaptive-Consistency together already approximate the whole proposal, so a concurrent-work collision is likely. Also, putting weighted (fractional, correlated) counts into a Dirichlet breaks multinomial conjugacy, so 'P(true mode)' and the VoI number are heuristics, not calibrated posteriors — the combination's theoretical selling point is shakier than the raw-count version it extends. Recommendation: drop all 'novel weighting' framing for DDWC (cite fitness sharing, ridge leverage, VecCISC up front), correct the false Adaptive-Consistency premise, and reposition the paper as an empirical Pareto study: does effective-vote-weighted Dirichlet-VoI stopping dominate Adaptive-Consistency, CISC, and VecCISC under identical token accounting? If it does not clearly dominate, there is no paper.

RISKS: Reviewer-recognition risk: DDWC's w_i = c_i / sum_j S_ij is textbook fitness sharing (Goldberg & Richardson 1987) and inverse-density/ridge-leverage weighting; anyone from ensembling/EC/RandNLA flags it immediately. Presenting it as a novel weight invites rejection. Cite fitness sharing, ridge leverage, and VecCISC up front.; Weight-collapse risk: with cosine similarity of LLM reasoning embeddings, S_ij is rarely near 0, so sum_j S_ij is nearly constant across i and w_i collapses toward uniform — DDWC degenerates back to plain SC. Highly sensitive to kernel scale/temperature/threshold; needs calibrated/sharpened kernels and a reported sensitivity sweep.; Discounting-backfire risk: correlated agreement among INDEPENDENT CORRECT chains is exactly the signal SC exploits. Down-weighting popular clusters can hand the win to a rare, confidently-wrong lone chain (one hallucination keeps full weight). Wrong-but-duplicated is real, but so is right-and-duplicated; net effect is empirical and may be negative on many benchmarks (prior weighted-SC gains are often small).; Anticipation/scoop risk on stopping: the Dirichlet + P(leader stable) rule IS Adaptive-Consistency, and the VoI/cost half is a crowded 2025-26 area (Pandora's-box, CITE, ConSol, MARS, Optimal Bayesian Stopping).
